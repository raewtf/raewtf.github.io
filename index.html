Featured Project

Labsylle

Coming Soon   •   Playdate

Labsylle's a cool word puzzle game, all about the "building blocks" of the English language. Swap around syllable blocks, two at a time, to assemble words — that's it! It'll keep you busy, too; with over 450 curated word puzzles, 100 puzzles worth of bonus modes that put a 'spin' on the usual gameplay, and a whole dictionary of Quik-Words to experience in 60-second bursts.
