# What's *RowBot Rally*?

*RowBot Rally* is a big game I made for the Playdate. In it, you play as a bunny, eager to anticipate in the Fish Bowl; a yearly boating competition that sparks excitement through the town. However, the nefarious Robo-X has been manufacturing so-called "RowBots", which have been progressively taking over the entire competition.

Armed with an abandoned scrapyard RowBot of your own, you set out to prove that organic beings can still win the Fish Bowl! ...After you get a little sidetracked, though, the goal changes. Have you got what it takes to track down the Shark Boss, stop Robo-X's plans, and give the Fish Bowl back to the people?! ...Well, you've gotta play the game to find out! ;P

# What of "Project Pesto"?

You might have heard one of my many teasings about a "Project Pesto"[^2] in the last year or so. That's this! I started working on it in an official capacity last June — and frankly, I'm surprised I was able to keep a secret that long...well, an open secret, at least.

With thanks to everybody reporting from Team PESTO, who I was able to confide in while I had to keep this thing a big surprise. Double-triple-quadruple thanks to everybody at Panic, who's already been providing immeasurable support to help the game out, and has been doing so since the beginning. The original game, nor this one, would be where it is today without them.
