On Payment

Prices are in USD. Payment is required after I finish the sketch, and of course no high-quality assets will be provided until after the commission's paid in full.


On Usage

My visual works are typically distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. This means that you can share, remix, or adapt anything I make for you, commercially or no; but you must provide credit, and you have to share the piece under the same terms.

Please DO NOT use my art for training in any large language models. That shit's icky.


More Info

I'll give you updates after each step in the process — after the rough sketch, the sketch, lines, colors, and shading (all if applicable). I don't mind revisions, but please keep it within reason — and speak up early on if you notice something you'd like changed! The earlier I know to fix it, the easier and faster it can get done. I reserve the right to deny any commission for any reason!
