Want some cool art?

As you may know by now, art is kinda my bag. But now I aim to get that bag, with... commissions! Are you in the market for some art, and maybe you've seen some of mine and thought, "perfect"? Well you're in luck, because you can throw some money at me in exchange for some of that goodness! Read on and, if you're interested, see the instructions below to let me know what you'd like. Slots are limited; inquire within!

I'll give you updates after each step in the process, with ample opportunity for tweaks. I don't mind revisions, but please keep it within reason — and speak up early on if you notice something you'd like changed! I reserve the right to deny any commission for any reason.
