On Privacy

When you send a form, your info will be slammed through Google servers. I'll be able to see what you request, but I don't intend to share it with anybody. As part of the commission process, I may ask you if you'd be fine with me posting your art to my socials, or using it to promote what I do (i.e. as an example of past work).


What About Usage?

My visual works are typically distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. This means that you can share, remix, or adapt anything I make for you, commercially or no; but you must provide credit, and you have to share the piece under the same terms.

Please DO NOT use my art for training in any large language models. That shit's icky.
