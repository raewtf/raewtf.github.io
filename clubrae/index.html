Club Rae

A newsletter for those with mailboxes.

Want seldom updates on what's happening in my world? Things I'm up to, or working on? Consider joining Club Rae — a free newsletter, for those who love actual mail! Fresh Rae updates, new projects, and perhaps even some secrets, typed out on a state-of-the-art electric typewriter and mailed straight to your door. When's the next one? You'll only find out once it shows up!

(This newsletter is only available to folks in the U.S. — sorry for the inconvenience!)

Slot Information

Slots are very limited — I've only got so much time...and stamps!! Signing up gets you letters for the next year. After that, your spot will open back up for someone else to claim — or you to re-claim! This is just so the pot has a chance of getting stirred a bit, and new people have a chance to join. Hopefully that works!

On Privacy

When you send this form, your info will be slammed through Google servers and saved to a private spreadsheet. I'll be able to see it, but I promise that I won't sell or share your info with anybody else. You'll only receive mail from me, whenever I send stuff out, and you can opt out at any time by using the opt-out form.


Some More Info

If you choose to receive letters, I'd really appreciate it if you kept any cool secrets you hear about ... secret! This is a fun thing I'm trying out, and I wouldn't like to have any beans spilled over someone being silly.

Letters are sent untracked, at random intervals (possibly seasonal?), and are meant to be a fun unannounced surprise in your mail every-so-often. I don't assume responsibility if it gets lost along the way, though that would be a bummer!!
